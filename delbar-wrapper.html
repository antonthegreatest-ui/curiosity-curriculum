DELBAR Wrapper

Give any resource a DELBAR shape

Drop the phase icons and chevrons into your own slides and sheets, or upload a resource you already like and get it back as a DELBAR worksheet, slide deck and task board.

🛡️
Anonymous by design. No sign-in, no cookies, nothing saved on our server.
  • Your file is read inside your browser. It is never uploaded or stored.
  • Only the lesson text is sent to the AI to build the phases. Images are not sent.
  • Before sending, the tool checks for names, dates of birth, pupil numbers and contact details, and offers to remove them.

Upload a resource you like

A worksheet, slide deck, PDF or Google Doc. The tool keeps your tasks, questions and key words, sorts them into the six DELBAR phases, and adds a short Do Now or Review if yours has none.

Google Docs or Slides? Use File → Download → Microsoft Word (.docx) or PowerPoint (.pptx), then drop it here. Or select all, copy, and use the Paste text tab.
