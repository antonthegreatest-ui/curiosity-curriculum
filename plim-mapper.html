PLIM Mapper

Put each target where it fits

Place a learner's PLIM targets in the DELBAR phases of any lesson, so every activity gives them a real chance to show progress.

🛡️
Anonymous by design. No sign-in, no cookies, nothing saved on our server.
  • The learner reference you type never leaves this device. It is only printed on your sheets.
  • Only the target wording and the lesson text are sent to the AI. Files are read inside your browser.
  • Mark personal targets as Staff only. They stay off anything a pupil or their peers can see.
Step 1
The learner's PLIM targets

Paste targets from the PLIMS Builder or any PLIM. Put each domain name on its own line, with its targets underneath.

Stays on this device. Never sent to the AI.
Printed on the teacher map.
Step 2
The lesson or resource

A worksheet, task board, slide deck or lesson plan. For a full personalised worksheet, wrap it in the DELBAR Wrapper first and press "Map PLIM targets".

Google Docs or Slides? Use File → Download → .docx or .pptx, then drop it here. Or select all, copy, and use the Paste text tab.
Takes about 15 seconds.
Step 3 · check and print
The target map

One target per phase at most. Change anything below, then press Update.

Sits beside the learner's own copy of the resource. They tick a face for each target in Review.
Staff-only targets appear on the teacher map only. Pupil sheets use the neutral wording. Whole-class slides carry no individual targets.
